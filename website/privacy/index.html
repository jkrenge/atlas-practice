Privacy policy

The short version: Atlas collects nothing. Your progress stays on your device.

Effective 23 November 2026

Who we are

Atlas, the app and this website, is made by Julian Krenge. When this policy says “we”, that's who it means.

You can reach us through the issue tracker on GitHub: github.com/jkrenge/atlas-practice/issues.

The app

We collect no data

Atlas doesn't collect, send, sell or share any personal data. There is no account and no sign-in. We never see your answers or your scores.

No tracking, no analytics, no ads

The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking code, and no third-party code that could do it for us. It doesn't track you across other apps or websites.

No network

Everything the app needs, maps, facts and flags included, ships inside it. Atlas works offline and doesn't connect to any server. Links in the app, such as fact sources, help, and this policy, open in your browser; the site you land on then handles your visit under its own privacy policy.

No permissions

Atlas doesn't ask for your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone. Pin questions use the pin you place on the map, not where you are.

What stays on your device

To be useful, the app remembers a few things in its own storage on your iPhone or iPad:

  • your settings: questions per round and the timer;
  • your practice scores for each map and way to play;
  • the map and mode you played last, so the home screen can offer it again.

This stays on the device. Like other app data, it can be part of the device backups you make with iCloud or your computer; those are between you and Apple. To delete your scores, open Settings and tap Reset progress. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Apple and the App Store

Apple handles downloads and updates under its own privacy policy. If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your device settings, Apple may give us aggregated usage figures and crash reports. They don't identify you, and we can't link them to you.

Children

Atlas collects no data from anyone, children included.

This website

Hosting

This site is a set of static pages hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. When you visit, GitHub processes technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages and keep the service secure. GitHub logs and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes, whether or not they are signed in to GitHub. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Legal basis: our legitimate interest in delivering this site reliably and securely (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). GitHub, Inc. is based in the USA and receives this data under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework; see the Data Privacy Framework section of GitHub's privacy statement.

No cookies, no analytics

The site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, trackers, external fonts or third-party scripts. Every file comes from the same place.

Links

Links to other sites, such as data sources, take you to pages with their own privacy policies.

If you contact us

Support runs through GitHub issues. Whatever you post there, including your GitHub username, is public and is processed by GitHub under GitHub's privacy statement. We read it only to answer you and to fix the app. You can edit or delete your own comments; ask us in the issue and we'll remove or lock an issue you opened. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, or Art. 6(1)(b) where your message concerns using the app.

If you live in the EU, the EEA or the UK, you have the right to access, correct and delete personal data we hold about you, to restrict or object to its use, to data portability, and to complain to a data protection authority. Since the app and this site give us no data about you, what you post in an issue is the only thing we could hold.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the effective date at the top. If Atlas ever starts collecting data, this page will say so before the app does.
